How It Works

One shipment, five checkpoints.

A fixed sequence we run on every order, so nothing reaches a port without a paper trail behind it.

01
Intake

Quote & sourcing brief

You share product specs and volumes; we return supplier options, landed cost estimates, and a lead-time forecast within 48 hours.

02
Production

Order placement & inspection

We place the PO, monitor production milestones, and schedule pre-shipment inspection before anything is packed for export.

03
Transit

Booking & freight

Carrier booked, container loaded under supervision, and full documentation issued — bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin.

04
Border

Customs clearance

Our brokers classify, declare, and clear the shipment at destination, handling duties and any inspections that arise.

05
Delivery

Final-mile & reconciliation

Goods delivered to warehouse or end customer, with a closing report reconciling cost, timeline, and any variances against the original quote.
